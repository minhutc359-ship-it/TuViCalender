# AstroAI editorial illustrations

Sáu ảnh WebP trong `public/assets/illustrations/` được tạo ngày 02/11/2026 bằng công cụ tạo ảnh tích hợp của OpenAI, sau đó crop về 1200×800 và nén WebP quality 76. Ảnh không chứa chữ; typography và nội dung được render bằng giao diện để giữ khả năng đọc, dịch và truy cập. Các ảnh được tái sử dụng có chủ đích trong portal/atlas để không tải trùng asset; lớp reveal, quỹ đạo và shimmer nằm trong CSS/motion, đồng thời tắt khi bật Reduce Motion.

Art direction chung: premium editorial fantasy, Celestial Feng Shui, painterly với ornamental linework rõ; midnight navy, indigo, antique gold, jade, ivory; không logo, watermark, chữ hoặc nhân vật có bản quyền.

## Prompt set

### `fusion-home.webp`

Wide AstroAI PWA home hero: Vietnamese Eastern cosmology and Western celestial symbolism in a midnight observatory garden; an antique-gold astrolabe merging a Bagua compass with a zodiac wheel, jade and rose-gold accents, balanced yin-yang composition, calm moonlight and responsive negative space.

### `eastern-knowledge.webp`

Wide Eastern knowledge portal: moonlit Vietnamese courtyard, floating jade Bagua compass above a bronze five-element circle, wood/fire/earth/metal/water expressed through natural materials, lantern gold and jade moonlight.

### `western-atlas.webp`

Wide Western astrology portal: deep observatory, silver and rose-gold zodiac wheel, crescent moon, planetary orbits, constellation lines and a blank antique atlas, cool restrained celestial light.

### `lunar-calendar.webp`

Wide Vietnamese lunisolar calendar scene: predawn river terrace, antique brass calendar disk with lunar phases and twelve zodiac animal silhouettes, bamboo shadow and jade seal, indigo-to-dawn lighting.

### `profile-cosmos.webp`

Wide personal profile scene without a real person: four symbolic pillars for year/month/day/hour, zodiac medallion, compass and blank profile card on an elegant study table under moon and stars.

### `chat-guide.webp`

Wide chatbot companion scene: a non-human celestial lantern and constellation spirit in a quiet reading nook, with jade Bagua and violet zodiac halos, blank journal and soft welcoming gold light.

Generated PNG masters remain outside the published application; only optimized WebP derivatives are included in the repository and offline manifest.
